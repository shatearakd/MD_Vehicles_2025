Methodology & limitations

01

Cleaned

Commas and currency symbols were removed before numeric conversion. Month abbreviations were converted to proper dates.

02

Aligned

Statewide sales and registrations are joined by month only where both sources contain the same date.

03

Preserved

Blank county labels after February 2024 continue the prior “NOT MD” series and are shown as “Not MD / Unspecified.”

04

Bounded

2026 is partial. Registration totals are stocks measured at month end; sales are monthly flows. Correlation is not causation.
